# Aria and the Very Important Hello — Page Image Prompts
### Matches prose draft 3. 29 single illustrated pages, each with its text lettered into the art.

29 illustrated pages, plus the title, copyright and dedication pages, make a standard 32-page picture book.

---

## How to use these

1. **Generate one page per image.** Every prompt is for a single square page. Never generate two pages in one image.
2. **Attach the approved reference sheets** listed under "Attach". The sheets are the source of truth. Where a sheet differs from the written Character Bible, follow the sheet.
3. **Paste the Global Prefix first**, then the page prompt.
4. **Generate pages 1 and 7 first.** Approve them, then attach page 1 as the **nursery/home reference** and page 7 as the **town reference** on later pages.
5. **Attach a typography reference on every page.** Pick one approved page whose lettering size looks right (page 1 or page 2 is a good choice) and attach it to every later prompt as "typography reference page". This is the strongest way to keep the font size the same: a written size alone drifts, because image tools tend to enlarge text to fill whatever space is free.
6. **Proofread the lettering on every page** against the text in this file. Image tools sometimes drop or misspell words, especially in longer passages. If a page keeps coming out wrong, generate it with the text area left empty and add the text afterwards in a design tool, using the same typeface.
7. **Butterfly rule:** the yellow butterfly appears ONLY on page 20 and page 29.
8. **Potcake rule:** the potcake is small and findable on the town pages where a prompt places him. He is never the focus, except on page 8.

## Global Prefix (paste at the start of every prompt)

> FORMAT: ONE single square picture-book page (1:1). One page only: never a double-page spread, never two panels side by side as separate pages, and no visible centre fold.
>
> STYLE: Traditional watercolour with fine, slightly broken sepia-black ink linework. Loose wet-in-wet washes in the background, more controlled painting on the characters. Visible cold-press paper texture, soft colour bleeding at the edges, warm natural light. Gently stylised proportions, expressive faces, warm and humorous. Hand-painted, never digital-looking.
>
> CHARACTERS: Match the attached character reference sheets exactly on every page: same face shape, skin tone, eye shape, hair texture and colour, glasses, jewellery, outfit and proportions. Do not invent new features, change outfits or age anyone up or down. Baby Aria is six months old: she sits or lies, never stands or walks, and has no teeth. Only the characters named in the page prompt appear, plus unnamed background townspeople where the prompt asks for them.
>
> LETTERING: Letter the page text given below EXACTLY as written: same words, spelling, capitals, punctuation and line breaks. Do not add, drop, reorder or paraphrase any word.
> - Typeface: one clean, warm, highly legible classic book serif, regular weight, dark sepia-black, left-aligned. The same typeface on every page.
> - Size is FIXED and identical on every page of the book. It does NOT grow to fill empty space and does NOT shrink to fit. Body text is a modest read-aloud size: capital letters about 2.5% of the page height, so roughly 25 lines of text would fill the full page from top to bottom. Lines run about 40 to 50 characters, with comfortable line spacing.
> - Match the size, typeface, weight, colour and line spacing of the attached typography reference page exactly.
> - If the text is short and the calm area is large, keep the text at the fixed size and leave the extra space empty. Never enlarge the letters.
> - Only a line the page prompt specifically marks as larger may be bigger, and never more than about 1.3 times the body size.
> - Keep the text block at least 6% of the page width away from every edge, on a calm, pale, lightly washed area with no busy detail behind the letters.
> - The page text is the ONLY lettering in the image: no other words, signs, labels, numbers, logos, speech bubbles or captions.
>
> AVOID: 3D rendering, glossy or plastic shading, cartoon vector look, anime eyes, photorealism, heavy black outlines, extra fingers or limbs, a yellow butterfly unless the page prompt asks for one.

# Cover and front and back matter

**Page plan (32 pages):**

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Title page |
| 2 | Dedication and copyright |
| 3–31 | Story pages 1–29 |
| 32 | Spare: an Adventure Club activity page, or About the Author |

**Lettering on these pages:**
- The **title** uses a warmer, hand-lettered display style. Everything else uses the same book serif as the story pages.
- **Long legal or barcode text should not be generated by an image tool.** The copyright page and back cover say which parts to letter in the image and which to typeset afterwards.

**Fill in before generating:**
- **Author name.** I used **Barbara Franks**. Change it if you want "Dr. Barbara Franks" or a pen name.
- **Series line.** I used **An Aria's Adventure Club Book** as plain lettering, because the series logo has not been redesigned yet. When the new watercolour logo exists, generate these pages with the series line left out and place the logo instead.
- **Publisher or imprint name, and the ISBN.** These are needed for the copyright page.
