# Aria's Adventure Club — Character Bible (Book 1)

Use this to generate one reference sheet per character before any page art. Generate the sheet, approve it, then feed the approved sheet back in as the image reference for every page that character appears on.

Skin-tone hex values are approximate targets, not exact. Check them against the photos.

---

## 1. Style block (paste at the start of EVERY prompt)

> Children's picture-book illustration in traditional watercolour with fine, slightly broken sepia-black ink linework. Loose wet-in-wet washes in the background, more controlled and detailed painting on the characters. Visible cold-press paper texture, soft colour bleeding at the edges, warm golden morning light. Gently stylised proportions: soft rounded forms, slightly large heads, expressive faces with large dark eyes and warm rosy cheeks. Hand-painted, warm, humorous and tender.

**Negative / avoid:** 3D render, glossy or plastic shading, cartoon vector look, anime eyes, photorealism, airbrushed gradients, heavy black outlines, text or lettering in the image.

**Series palette:** sea-glass teal, coral, bougainvillea pink, yellow-elder yellow, sand, faded pastel house colours (lime, peach, sky blue), crisp school-uniform white and navy.

## 2. Reference sheet layout (paste after the character description)

> Character reference sheet on a plain warm off-white paper background. Full body shown in front view, three-quarter view, side view and back view, all at the same scale. Below, a row of six facial expressions: [list]. Small colour swatches for skin, hair and outfit. The same character, identical features and outfit, in every view.

## 3. Aria (6 months)

**Who she is on the page:** A watcher who turns into a giggler. Her two signature looks are "the stare" and "the gummy grin." The book depends on readers recognising both.

**Build:** A six-month-old baby. Head about one-third of her total sitting height. Very chubby, with creases at the wrists and thighs, a round tummy and short dimpled hands and feet. Sits up with a slight forward lean.

**Skin:** Deep warm brown with a red-brown undertone (approx. #7A4A30). Rosy-brown flush on the cheeks. A shade deeper than Mama's.

**Face:**
- Very round face, with the widest point at the full apple cheeks. Small soft chin.
- Large, round, very dark brown eyes, almost black, with big white catchlights. Upper lids slightly heavy, which gives her serious face a "studying" look.
- Short, soft eyebrows, usually lifted a little.
- Small, broad button nose.
- Small, full, bow-shaped lips.
- Small ears with tiny gold stud earrings.

**Hair:** Short, soft, jet-black tight curls, slightly longer on the crown, with the soft hairline visible. Two styles:
- **(a)** one tiny top-knot puff with a small bow
- **(b)** two tiny pigtail puffs with matching small bows

Her bows always match her outfit.

**No birthmark:** Do not draw any birthmark, mole or skin mark on Aria. Her legs are clear.

**Book 1 outfits:**
- **Town day (spreads 4–14):** Coral ribbed sleeveless romper with fluttered sleeves, a matching small coral bow on her top-knot (style a), barefoot.
- **Crib mornings (spread 1 and final page):** Soft white cotton sleep romper with a tiny yellow-elder flower print, two pigtail puffs with white bows (style b).

**Props:** A striped sea-glass-teal and white stroller with a sunshade.

**Expressions for her sheet:**
1. The stare: serious, brows slightly raised, mouth small and closed, studying.
2. The gummy grin: huge open mouth, no teeth, eyes squeezed into crescents, cheeks pushed up.
3. "BAH!": leaning forward, mouth wide open.
4. Shy burrow: face pressed into a shoulder, one eye peeking out.
5. Delighted: arms and legs flung out, mouth an O.
6. The wave: one arm up, fingers splayed, slightly wobbly, tongue poking out in concentration.

## 4. Mama

**Who she is on the page:** Unhurried, warm and quietly funny. She greets everyone with eye contact. She never lectures. She bends down to a child's height.

**Build:** A tall-ish, full-figured, curvy grown woman with soft rounded shoulders. She carries Aria on her hip naturally.

**Skin:** Warm medium brown with a golden undertone (approx. #A8704B). Lighter than Aria's.

**Face:**
- Round-oval face with full cheeks and high, rounded cheekbones.
- Warm brown eyes with long lashes and softly arched, defined brows.
- Small rounded nose. Full lips.
- Her most recognisable expression: a serene, closed-mouth smile with her eyes lowered toward Aria.

**Hair (her signature):** Long, very full curly microlocs (sisterlocks): hundreds of fine, uniform, pencil-thin locs, each with a crinkled, curly texture along its length and curled, slightly frizzy ends. NOT loose curls, coils or twists: each loc should read as a distinct, matte, slightly textured strand. Dark brown-black at the roots, fading to warm caramel-copper toward the ends. The locs fall well past her shoulders with a lot of volume, and the small square parting grid of the sisterlocks is faintly visible at the scalp near her off-centre part. Two looks:
- **Town:** worn down
- **Home / mornings:** the locs gathered into a high bun on the crown, with a few curly locs falling loose to frame her face

**Accessories (always):**
- A stack of four or five thin gold bangles on her right wrist
- Medium gold hoop earrings
- Soft almond-shaped nude-pink nails

**Glasses (always):** warm brown, softly rounded rectangular frames, worn every day and in every scene. Draw them on every view and every expression on her reference sheet.

**Book 1 outfits:**
- **Town:** A loose butter-cream linen blouse with billowy sleeves rolled to the elbow, light-wash jeans and tan sandals. A woven shoulder bag holds her list and the light-bill envelope.
- **Mornings:** A soft peach house dress, hair in the high bun.

**Expressions for her sheet:**
1. Serene smile, looking down at Aria
2. Bright open smile, greeting a neighbour
3. Trying not to laugh: lips pressed, cheeks lifted
4. Gentle concern, looking at Kai
5. Laughing
6. Delighted surprise: the final-page "hello to you, too"

## 5. Kai (about 7)

**Who he is on the page:** A boy holding very still while the world moves around him. His arc runs from frozen, to a smile, to frozen again, to going anyway. His body language does most of the work.

**Build:** Slim and a little gangly, with knobbly knees and thin arms.

**Skin:** Rich dark brown with a cool undertone (approx. #5A3A28).

**Face:**
- Large dark eyes, and brows that tilt up in the middle when he is worried.
- Slightly prominent ears.
- A missing front tooth that shows only when he finally smiles.

**Hair:** A fresh, very short low cut with a crisp line-up: an obvious first-day-of-school haircut.

**Uniform:**
- Crisp white short-sleeved shirt with a school crest on the pocket
- Navy shorts
- Navy-and-gold striped clip-on tie, slightly crooked
- White socks pulled high
- Shiny brand-new black shoes

**Backpack (key prop):** An enormous bright red backpack, far too big for him, reaching the backs of his knees. A tiny toy-dinosaur keyring hangs from the zip. This is a "find it" detail that can return in later books.

**Expressions for his sheet:**
1. Withdrawn: shoulders up, both hands gripping the straps, eyes on his shoes
2. Peeking sideways
3. Mouth twitch: a smile he is trying not to have
4. First real smile, gap-toothed
5. Laughing
6. Frozen and worried at the car door

## 6. Kai's Auntie

**Who she is on the page:** Kind, practical and short on time. Her nurse's scrubs explain, without words, why she cannot take Kai to school herself.

A grown woman in her forties with medium-dark brown skin and short natural hair in a neat low bun. She wears teal hospital scrubs, a hospital ID lanyard and a plain wristwatch, which she keeps checking. Comfortable white trainers.

Key gesture: her hand flat on Kai's back, left there.

Expressions: checking the time, warm greeting, laughing, quiet support.

---

## 7. The girl in the carpool (about 7, unnamed for now)

Deep brown skin. Two thick plaits tied with white ribbon bows. A confident, gap-toothed grin. Same school as Kai: a navy pinafore over a white blouse with the same crest. Her school bag is covered in patches.

Key gesture: pulling her bag off the seat, then patting the empty space twice.

---

## 8. Mr. Rolle (shopkeeper)

In his seventies. Tall and lean, slightly stooped. Dark brown skin, close-cropped white hair, a neat white moustache and deep laugh lines. Reading glasses in his shirt pocket. He wears a short-sleeved pale blue button shirt, grey trousers and worn leather sandals, and leans on a straw broom.

His shop is a small painted wooden storefront with a hand-lettered sign, ROLLE'S GENERAL STORE. Keep the lettering out of generated images and add it afterwards.

---

## 9. Miss Pearl (fruit vendor)

In her sixties. Round and comfortable, with medium-brown skin. She wears a yellow-and-orange print head wrap, gold-rimmed glasses on a beaded chain and a floral apron with deep pockets. She sits on a stool under a faded umbrella at a wooden fruit stand piled with mangoes, sapodillas and bananas, beside a small chalk price board.

She has a big laugh: head back, hand on her chest.

---

## 10. The Potcake

A medium-small, lean neighbourhood dog with a short sandy-ginger coat, a white chest blaze and white "socks" on his front paws. One ear stands up and the other folds over. His tail curls over his back, and he has expressive tan eyebrow dots.

His personality: a self-appointed escort. He appears on every town spread. Children should be able to find him every time.

---

## 11. The yellow butterfly

A small cloudless sulphur butterfly with plain lemon-yellow wings. Keep it simple and tiny. It appears only on spread 10 and the final page.

## 13. Family cast

Aria's father does not appear in the series. Family members come into a story only when the story needs them.

**Family resemblance to carry across everyone:** warm brown skin in a range of shades, round soft cheeks and a wide, open smile. Mama and Grandma both wear brown glasses, but in different shapes so they never blur together.

**Tone note for all the adult women:** the photos show full glam makeup. In the watercolour style, suggest it lightly with defined brows, a hint of lashes and rosy or berry lips. That keeps each woman recognisable without looking out of place in a children's book.

### 13a. Grandma (appears in Book 1, spreads 2 and 3)

**Who she is on the page:** Composed, dignified and warm. She is the steady one, and she laughs readily.

**Build:** Medium height, soft and sturdy, with rounded shoulders.

**Skin:** Light-medium warm brown with a golden undertone (approx. #B98A6A). Lighter than Mama's.

**Face:** Round face with soft full cheeks and a broad, warm smile that shows her teeth. Kind, crinkling eyes.

**Glasses (always):** Bold, dark tortoiseshell-brown rectangular frames. They are squarer and darker than Mama's softer, rounder frames.

**Hair:** Short, chin-length, springy dark-brown curls with volume on top, softly framing her face.

**Jewellery:** A fine pearl necklace, silver drop-hoop earrings and rings on both hands.

**Signature style:** Neat and put-together. A deep wine-red top, a black tailored jacket, black trousers and black strappy flat sandals. Handbag over her forearm.

**Book 1:** Coming up the front steps with her handbag (spread 2), then laughing at Aria's "wave" beside Mama (spread 3).

**Expressions for her sheet:** warm greeting, delighted laugh, "She's waving!" (pointing, thrilled), fond look at Aria over her glasses, mock-serious, gentle listening.

### 13b. Aunt Gail (photo: Anita, leopard-print top)

**Who she is on the page:** The glamorous, fun aunt. She is Cousin Amy's mother.

**Skin:** Warm medium-light brown.

**Face:** Oval face, high cheekbones, strongly arched defined brows, almond eyes, a bright closed-lip smile and glossy pink lips.

**Hair (her signature):** Long, sleek, straight black-brown hair with a deep side part, swept over one shoulder. Chunky honey-blonde highlights frame her face, and one side is tucked behind her ear.

**Jewellery:** Large chunky textured gold hoops and a silver link watch.

**Signature style:** Bold prints, especially leopard, with a black crossbody bag.

### 13c. Aunty Ann (photo: Philippa Anne, white top with black bow)

**Who she is on the page:** Cool, quietly confident and a little mysterious.

**Skin:** Light-medium warm brown.

**Face:** Soft oval face, defined brows, gentle hooded eyes, a small knowing smile and dark berry-red lips.

**Hair (her signature):** Pulled back sleek and tight from the hairline, with laid edges, into a very long, very full blown-out ponytail with a kinky-straight texture that falls over one shoulder.

**Jewellery:** Large thin gold hoops and a silver chain with a heart pendant.

**Signature style:** Classic pieces with one detail, such as a white top with a black bow.
