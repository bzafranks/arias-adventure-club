# Aria and the Very Important Hello
### Aria's Adventure Club, Book 1. Rhyming version, draft 1 (one couplet per page)

Aria's age: 6 months. Meter: anapestic (da-da-DUM), four beats per line, written for read-aloud.
Page count: 29 text pages. This fits a standard 32-page picture book once the title, copyright and dedication pages are added.

Recurring "find it" details for children:
- **The potcake.** A scruffy neighbourhood potcake dog. Aria stares at him on page 2, and he quietly follows Mama and Aria all over town. He is on every town page.
- **The yellow butterfly.** The narrator says there is no butterfly. The art disagrees on page 15, and the butterfly comes back on the last page.

---

**1.**
"Good morning, my sunshine!" sang Mama each day,
and Aria kicked out a hello right away.
*Art: Crib by jalousie windows, early light. Aria's legs are a happy blur.*

**2.**
For Grandma, a squeal. For the potcake, a stare.
For the baby in the mirror, a "BAH!" in the air.
*Art: Three small vignettes: Grandma coming up the front steps; the potcake at the screen door, staring right back; Aria nose-to-nose with the mirror.*

**3.**
And lately she'd practised a brand-new hello:
a wave (well, a flap) that went wobbly and slow.

**4.**
It looked like she grabbed for a butterfly's wing,
but there wasn't a butterfly there. Not a thing.
*Art: Aria mid-grab, cheeks puffed with effort. Mama and Grandma trying very hard not to laugh.*

**5.**
One morning, with Mama, she went into town
in the stroller with stripes and the sunshade pulled down.
*Art: Mama with a short list and a bill envelope in hand. Painted wooden houses, a yellow elder in bloom, a corner shop. The potcake trots behind them.*

**6.**
"Good morning!" said Mama to Mr. Rolle, sweeping.
"Good morning, Miss Pearl!" where the mangoes were heaping.

**7.**
"Good mornings" for everyone, near ones and far:
for the bus, for the baker, the man in the car.
*Art: The jitney driver lifts two fingers off the wheel. The baker waves flour-dusted hands. Every adult greets back.*

**8.**
And Aria watched them with eyes open wide.
So many hellos! She was bubbling inside.
*Art: Aria leaning out of the stroller, head swivelling, mouth a perfect O.*

**9.**
Then she spotted a boy on a bench by the wall,
with a backpack so big that he looked very small.
*Art: Kai, about 7, in a crisp new uniform with an enormous backpack. His aunt sits beside him. Town bustles in every direction. Kai is the only still thing on the page.*

**10.**
He looked up at Aria. She looked at him too.
Then he looked at the ground, at the tip of his shoe.

**11.**
"Good morning!" said Mama. His auntie said, "Hey!
His ride's coming soon. It's Kai's new-school day."

**12.**
Now, Aria knew nothing of school or its bell,
but she knew about NEW. She knew NEW very well.

**13.**
Some new was a giggle. Some new was a fright,
the kind where you hide in your mama's neck, tight.
*Art: Two memory vignettes in soft vignette edges: Aria shrieking with joy at her first splash in a basin; Aria burying her face in Mama's neck when a stranger leaned in too close.*

**14.**
When Kai looked again, she smiled big and smiled wide,
a gummy-gum grin with not one tooth inside.

**15.**
Then up went her arm for her brand-new hello.
She grabbed for a butterfly. (There wasn't one, though.)
*Art: There IS one: a tiny yellow butterfly just above Aria's hand. Nobody notices it except the reader.*

**16.**
Kai blinked. Then his smile came up slow, like the sun.
He waved back at Aria: a real, proper one.

**17.**
Kai came a bit closer and said, "Hi, Aria."
And she shouted right back, "GAH-BAH-GOO-BLAH-ria!"

**18.**
Kai laughed. Auntie laughed. Mama laughed. And then
Aria laughed, so they all laughed again.
*Art: Miss Pearl laughing behind her mangoes. The potcake's tail is a blur.*

**19.**
Then BEEP-BEEP! went a car, with its back door swung wide,
and Kai's smile slid away and went somewhere to hide.

**20.**
He stood up. He stopped. He held tight to his strap.
His feet didn't move. Not a step. Not a tap.

**21.**
"New things can feel big," Mama said to him, low.
Auntie squeezed his hand gently. She didn't say "Go."

**22.**
A girl in the car moved her bag. Just so.
She patted the seat. Pat-pat. Hello.
*Art: A girl in the same uniform, two plaits with bows, sliding her bag off the back seat.*

**23.**
Kai looked at his auntie. Kai looked at the seat.
Kai looked back at Aria, still waving (with feet!).
*Art: Aria waving with both arms and both feet at once.*

**24.**
"Bye, Aria!" Kai waved. Then he climbed up inside,
with a tummy that flip-flopped the whole of the ride.
*Art: Kai's face at the car window, still worried, one hand raised.*

**25.**
Then Mama kissed Aria's nose, soft and slow.
"Now that was a very important hello."

**26.**
Some hellos are spoken. Some hellos are smiled.
Some are waved by a baby, all wobbly and wild.

**27.**
Some are loud as a squeal. Some are soft as a pat.
Some ride all the way to new school. Imagine that!
*Art: Kai at the door of his new classroom, the girl with the plaits beside him. He gives a small, careful wave to a child inside. The hello has travelled with him.*

**28.**
Next morning: "Good morning, my sunshine!" And whoa,
up came a small hand. A flap. A hello.

**29.**
"Well, hello to you, too!" Mama said with a grin,
and the sun, like a big yellow hello, came in.
*Art: Morning light through the jalousies. The yellow butterfly sits on the windowsill.*
